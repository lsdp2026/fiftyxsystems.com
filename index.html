FAQ

Common questions.

What is expedited delivery?

A dedicated, time-sensitive delivery. Your shipment goes directly from pickup to destination instead of waiting at a freight terminal or riding on a shared route.

How do I get a price?

Call 281-456-4926 with the pickup location, drop location, a rough size and weight, and when it needs to arrive. We'll give you a straight quote.

Do you work with any business?

Yes. Fifty X Systems is an independent courier and delivery company. If you have something that needs to be delivered, give us a call.

Is there anything you can't deliver?

Items that can't be safely secured on our equipment, or shipments that require special permits or licensing we don't hold. If we're not the right fit, we'll tell you up front.
